# Ethics and scope

- Everything runs in an isolated VirtualBox internal network owned by the team.
- No scanning, password guessing or phishing against any system or person outside the lab.
- Fictitious accounts and data only, consistent with Ecuador's Personal Data Protection Law (LOPDP, 2021).
- The repository publishes detection rules, hardening and synthetic logs. It does not publish attack tooling,
  wordlists or phishing templates.
